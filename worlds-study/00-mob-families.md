# The mob families

All 383 mobs, grouped by what the icon actually shows — the grouping that decides which biome a
mob belongs in. `W` is the world it already lives in (`D` = dungeon-only seat). **This map never
moves a mob between worlds**; it only says what habitat that mob is asking for.

Families were cut by name root and confirmed against the models: 241 of the 383 mobs share a
`.glb` with another mob (a recolour), and those groups landed inside one family almost everywhere —
e.g. `koa`, `koaletin`, `ditch_koaletin`, `koard`, `koa_the_bloated` and `aw_lightless_frog` are
literally the same blue toad. That reuse is the strongest evidence of what a family IS.
