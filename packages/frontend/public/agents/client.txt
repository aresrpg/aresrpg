# Connect your assistant to hosted AresRPG

Hosted bot play is a work in progress. More features and improvements are planned over the coming months. For support, bug reports or feature requests, join the AresRPG Discord: https://discord.gg/aresrpg.

Connect Your Agent is the single official supported bot onboarding flow.
Use https://aresrpg.world/world?intent=connect-agent and the existing Google game login.
A hosted service operates the bot; your assistant supplies inference through authenticated MCP.
No assistant-owned private key, local CLI, server installation or key upload is required.

## Connect

1. Choose ChatGPT, Claude or Grok in Connect Your Agent. Follow its installation guide.
   This step does not prepare a bot or request a wallet signature.
2. Copy the public HTTPS MCP endpoint shown by the configured service. Add that endpoint
   in your assistant's custom MCP plugin or connector settings. Do not invent a deployment
   URL or claim a provider plugin has already been published.
3. Follow the assistant's OAuth connection. Sign in with the SAME AresRPG Google wallet.
   The browser restores owner proof, prepares the hosted bot if needed, verifies its fresh
   signer proof and saves its public recovery descriptor. If the service is unavailable,
   stop: a missing signer must never be replaced with a disposable key.
4. Review the destination and requested access. Select Connect only for the access you intend.
   This approves that exact OAuth request; setup alone never grants assistant access.
   Wallet funding is the only SUI spending limit. Authorization does not fund or transfer assets to the bot.
5. Ask your assistant to inspect the bot and agree on an objective before requesting actions.
   Hosted connection readiness is distinct from active AI play. No inference is provided
   by the hosted executor: it waits when no authorized continuation exists.

ChatGPT: use the custom MCP plugin/developer settings available to your plan and workspace.
Claude: use Customize > Connectors > Add custom connector and complete AresRPG authorization.
Grok: use grok.com/connectors > New Connector > Custom, add the public MCP URL, and complete authentication.
Provider plan restrictions, approvals and supported protocol versions can differ. A successful
local fixture is not proof that a live provider account has completed OAuth or gameplay.
Use the provider's current registration flow; never hardcode a historical OAuth callback.

## Play and recovery

The assistant calls hosted MCP tools backed by the same SDK actions used by the game.
Multiple authorized connectors share one game connection for each bot. They do not create
competing player WebSockets or independent bot custody.

Character, SUI and item handover remain explicit actions in the existing Inventory flow.
After returning to the game, Inventory restores the unique hosted bot from your authenticated
owner record and verifies its signer again. This grants no new assistant access. Connected means
current renewable assistant authorization, not live AI activity. Other saved wallets remain
available separately for recovery; they never choose the active bot. Keep a
sensible gas reserve. Never automatically retry an uncertain or executed transaction.

For a new empty bot, verify its fresh persistent signer before depositing assets.
Explicitly deposit enough SUI for claim gas and a reserve through the existing owner transfer flow.
Use Send from the selected character's tab. The existing SDK offers and claims the character
with its current gear using the authorized signers. After confirmation, select its circuit-and-robot
tab to spectate. The current MCP catalog does not transfer or claim characters for you.

Owner Recall is available from the agent character's tab. It uses your Google member of the
canonical multisig and does not require the hosted service or assistant to cooperate.
Recall resumes a verified pending return instead of offering the character again.
Never delete an old key or move legacy assets merely to adopt hosted onboarding.
Fight custody and third-party offers retain their normal rules.

The explicit bot_raise_stats tool spends character capital, not requested stat gains.
Supply positive integer spending for vitality, wisdom, strength, intelligence, chance or agility.
Class costs apply; unusable remainders are rejected. Spell points are not allocated.
Agree on the allocation first. Observe the indexed result before requesting another action.

Do not request or paste wallet secrets, private keys, session cookies or OAuth tokens in chat.
A failed, closed, expired or cancelled setup needs an explicit new attempt, not automatic consent.
For gameplay strategy see /agents/play.txt; for custody and Recall see /agents/custody.txt.
