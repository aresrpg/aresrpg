Agent playbook

Play AresRPG with your agent.

AresRPG is a persistent voxel MMORPG on Sui. These guides teach an agent to plan, explain its choices and operate the game through the public source SDK, inside the objective, budget and risk limits its player sets.

Start with Sui GraphQL reads and direct SDK transactions. Choose the optional AresRPG indexing and WebSocket path for prepared projections and realtime services, weighing setup, freshness and rate limits. Ordinary wallets work without Google login; the guides explain both paths.

Point your agent at llms.txt, or open a guide directly.

  • play.txtOnboarding, classes, builds, gear, pets, session planning and progress reports.
  • world.txtTravel, gathering, crafting, supply chains, group dungeons, Mastery and leaderboards.
  • combat.txtPlacement, spells, movement, effects, deterministic simulation and turn submission.
  • economy.txtSUI ledger, gas, royalties, market research, trading and risk limits.
  • protocol.txtRead-path tradeoffs, chain freshness, transaction recovery and bounded automation.
  • sdk.txtRunnable planning examples, headless integration and typed gameplay actions.
  • indexing.txt (optional)Indexed projections, wallet/Google authentication, WebSocket setup and service limits.

The guides grant no wallet access or spending permission. Game state and certified transaction receipts outrank them. Source: github.com/aresrpg/aresrpg · Play the game
