# v2.2.0 — the frozen frontier (2026-08-30)

Yakutia opens its gates, every class completes its spellbook, and the creatures fighting back now
play by their own rules.

## New content

- **Yakutia opens at level 20.** Cross six distinct frozen biomes to gather the next resources for
  every profession and face their protectors. Roaming fauna will arrive in a later expedition.
- **Every class completes its spellbook.** New attacks, sacrifices, poisons, movement tools, buffs,
  and critical effects create distinct builds and stronger class synergies.
- **Creatures gain identities.** Protectors mirror their profession and element, while each monster
  family now fights with its own tactics — including bosses that demand a real plan.

## New features

- **Dungeons open.** Their inhabitants grow more dangerous with distance from safety.
- **Adventure together.** Friends, parties, direct trades, and the Kolizeum arena connect players
  beyond the open world.
- **Smarter enemies spend their whole turn.** High-level monsters can chain spells when their AP
  allows it instead of stopping after one cast.
