# v2.4.0 — KARES, new cities, and seasonal rankings

More places to explore, new ways to earn rewards, and more reasons to return to your characters.

## KARES joins the adventure

- A dedicated launch page brings the KARES sale, token claims, and refunds into one place.
- The new **KARES** page lets you stake, claim accrued KARES and SUI rewards, and withdraw your
  stake. Game and external wallets have separate balances and controls.

## Three cities and their dungeons

- Explore **Thebes, the Ruins, and Fuwage**, each with its own layout and dungeon: Gilded Lorito,
  Tangled Aftermath, and Ivory Rampart. New creatures include Nook, elemental Loritos, Bramble,
  Tinker, and Wild Boar.
- Cities now appear on the compass with their direction and distance. Arrival banners, city
  potions, and the new **Recall Potion** make finding your way—and returning—easier.

## Mastery quests and leaderboards

- Choose a world and receive a **dungeon Mastery quest**. Earn points for completing it, then
  spend them on crates and reset scrolls. Saved points remain yours when you skip a day. Mastery
  rewards can also be bought with KARES, which are burned when spent.
- **Seasonal leaderboards** track combat, dungeon clears, professions, gathering, exploration,
  trading, Kolizeum results, and pet feeding. Browse the top 100, find your own rank, and revisit
  previous seasons.

## Your look, your companions

- Dedicated **cosmetic hat and cloak slots** let you change your appearance while keeping your
  regular equipment bonuses.
- New pets, crates, and giftcard claims expand the reward collection. Pet feeding now has clearer
  confirmation, animated reactions, and immediate stat feedback.

## Reworked runeforging

- Runeforging is available from **profession level 1**. Repair negative stats, restore lost AP or
  MP, and work with overmages and exotic bonuses through the puits system.
- Forging history now shows every stat lost and the correctly displayed puits balance. Rune
  applications remain a single transaction and no longer grant profession XP.

## Smoother everyday play

- Clearer profession level-ups, consumable descriptions, item inspection, and encyclopedia
  filters make progression easier to follow.
- More stable long-distance exploration and graphics fallback, plus fixes for stuck gathering,
  fight endings, inventory updates, and marketplace state when switching accounts.
