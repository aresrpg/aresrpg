**Your next quest reward? Gathering on autopilot. 🤖**

Finish every beginner quest and unlock **gathering automation**. Pick a resource, press Start, then browse your gear or check the market while your character gets to work. Collapse the controls to keep your screen clear—progress and Stop stay within reach.

This is just the beginning. Automation is part of what AresRPG is becoming, with more features to come.

**A better eye for a bargain 💰**

Stackable items get price history, so you can compare past sales before buying or setting your price. The 24h and 30d volume badges also show recorded sales sooner; **≥** means the available history doesn't cover the full window yet.

**Less clicking, more adventuring**

- Shift-click several pieces of equipment and crush them together after reviewing your selection.
- See your gear's actual stats in item details, with a cleaner equipment layout.
- Resize chat in fights just like you do in the world. Your size is saved between visits.
- Enemy turns play properly even when your device clock is off, with fewer waits after the finishing blow.
- Keep the music going while you browse other pages. Oceans show up blue on the minimap, too.

**Bring your friends 🌏**

Simplified Chinese, Russian, Vietnamese and Korean join the adventure—**10 languages** in total. We've filled translation gaps across gear, professions, spells and combat, and added a language dropdown that won't stretch your sidebar.

The beginner journal also gives clearer directions, and your wallet and fullscreen controls are easier to reach.
