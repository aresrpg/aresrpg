# v1.12.47 — coop keeps its promises

- **Predictions retire by claim** — an unrelated receipt can never purge your pending
  actions: no more HP rollbacks, "board was corrected" toasts on healthy boards, or
  dead mobs reviving. True mispredictions resolve as one honest forward correction.
- **Drafted casts survive commit** — every cast validates from where the caster stands
  at its point in the sequence, and ground-targeted casts (traps, glyphs) never
  target-revalidate. Placed traps stay placed; the yajin kit works.
- **Party invites enroll one character** — inviting a friend no longer sweeps your own
  alts into the party as camera-locked ghosts; bringing alts stays an explicit choice.
- **The turn timer is honest** — the countdown matches the real auto-commit moment, no
  auto-pass exists, and self-cast buffs can't be false-dropped at the deadline.
- **Equip refusals explain themselves** — level gates disable with the reason at hover;
  feedback fires instantly instead of after the network round-trip.
- **Victory card fits 6v6** — half-height rows, two-column rosters, dim visibility state.
- Coop visibility scope fixed (personal run ids no longer hide dungeon-mates), the
  local mob-icon residue gate, and the fight-pipeline architecture anchored in docs.
