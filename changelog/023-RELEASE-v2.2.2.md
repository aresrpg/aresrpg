# Parties, clearer fights, and smoother play

Group combat is smoother, clearer, and much easier to manage across several owned characters.

## Better parties and multi-character play

- Nearby owned characters join immediately. Late arrivals can still enter while placement is open.
- **Ready all** prepares every owned fighter with visible transaction progress.
- Fight rewards are collected across all owned participants with one clear progress display.
- Party followers retain their real positions across tab switches, reconnects, and fight entry.

## Clearer, faster combat

- The compact horizontal turn order shows portraits, health, elapsed time, effects, and resistances.
- A selected spell can target a fighter directly from their turn card as well as from the board.
- Fighter nameplates now stay focused on identity, level, and health.
- Critical highlights now appear only when the spell actually has a critical branch.
- Invisible fighters no longer block line of sight. Traps and glyphs disappear with their owner.
- Character crowds are substantially smoother without breaking hair, equipment, or animations.

## Guidance, sound, and interface polish

- New contextual tutorials introduce the world, fights, equipment, stats, spells, professions, and
  runeforging.
- Footsteps, biome and fight music, responsive stat panels, and testnet funding received more polish.
- Marketplace newcomers now receive a clear first-visit warning before trading player-priced items.
- Fight results, placement controls, errors, and multi-character progress are clearer and sturdier.
