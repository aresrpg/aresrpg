# v2.1.0 — the world comes alive (2026-08-23)

The world is no longer just scenery: it fights back, it grows, and it trades.

## New content

- **Packs roam the world.** Groups of creatures wander the land under one banner — walk up to
  one and attack it right where it stands. No arenas, no loading: the fight happens in the
  world, on the ground you were just walking on.
- **The world grows resources.** Ore veins, wild plants, and grain now grow out in the open.
  Gather them if your job level and tool are up to it — but watch yourself: a protector may
  ambush you mid-harvest.
- **Star gates.** Portals link the worlds — step through to travel, and scout unexplored zones
  to reveal the monsters and resources living there.

## New features

- **The marketplace opens.** Browse what other players are selling, list your own items, collect
  your proceeds, and follow your sales history — player-to-player trade is live.
- **Fights end with a verdict.** A result screen shows your loot and your level-ups the moment
  the last blow lands — and a duel you regret can now be called off before it starts.
- **All six characters, one world.** Your whole roster now stays in the world at once, each one
  right where you left them.
