# v1.12.40 — fights that behave

- Mobs visibly play their turns — no more frozen boards while the fight silently advances.
- Turn-order cards track HP live through every action instead of waiting for your END TURN.
- Killed mobs stay dead: the die → reappear → die again replay is gone, locked by a recorded-fight test.
- Kill the last mob and your turn commits itself — right after the spell's effects and the death animation finish. No button hunt at the moment of victory.
- Hovering and clicking in fights targets the board cell only — a big mob's body can no longer block the cell behind it.
- The victory card tells the truth: real loot icons, the item's actual name and characteristics on hover, and the fight's duration.
- Hundreds of item icons return across the encyclopedia and inventory — the game now derives icon names from chain truth instead of a build-time list that production never had.
