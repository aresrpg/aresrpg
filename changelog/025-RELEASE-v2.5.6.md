# AresRPG update — combat, crafting & trading

AresRPG is now on Sui mainnet, with improvements to dungeon runs, everyday progression, and the marketplace.

**⚔️ Combat & dungeons**

- Prepare between dungeon rooms: heal, change equipment, and adjust stats or spells before the next fight.
- Character level-ups now restore full HP.
- Forfeiting on your turn correctly advances combat instead of leaving the fight stuck.
- Improved starting-cell selection and placement countdowns, including recovery when another player takes your chosen cell.
- Fight and dungeon reward collection now estimates gas instead of requiring a fixed 1 SUI reserve.
- Failed reward collection now shows a retry action even when only one character is involved.
- Added **8 combat boards** and dungeon-key drops for the Aragne and Fuwa families.

**🔨 Crafting & gathering**

- Added **Max** beside crafting Attempts. It selects the most attempts your crafter’s available ingredients allow.
- Healing items now offer **Consume 1** or **Consume until full HP**.
- Profession details show your current gathering time and the next speed improvement.
- Corrected the runeforging tutorial: it unlocks at **profession level 1**, not 70.

**💰 Marketplace**

- Equipment now shows **one item per listing row**, with an icon revealing its exact stats and weapon damage.
- Stackable items keep the cheapest offers for each lot size.
- Added a **24h volume** badge showing public marketplace sales in SUI, before fees.
- Improved item tooltips in both Buy and Sell, including preventing tooltips from blocking clicks.

# AresRPG update — your account & adventure

**👛 Wallets & identity**

- Choose your **SuiNS name in Settings**. Point the name to your game wallet; `name@name` displays as `@name`.
- Improved Google sign-in recovery and recognition of returning players’ existing game wallets.
- Expired wallet sessions now return you to sign-in cleanly.
- Switch between authorized external wallets and accounts from a dropdown across Airdrops, KARES, and the launch page.
- Account switching is disabled while a claim or wallet transaction is pending.
- Improved wallet controls, sidebar access, and links from the KARES launch page back to the game.

**🎁 Community rewards & rankings**

- The **Airdrops** page now explains eligibility and claim steps for community rewards, physical cards, and Hytale rewards.
- Includes Aftermath Eggs, SWEEBS, selected Suifren and Vaporeon holders, and Prime Machin NFT gifts. Check each campaign’s snapshot rules.
- Added information for **Hytale top-100 veteran rewards** and the **Mark of the Unbroken** supporter title.
- Leaderboards now follow **monthly resets**, with a reset countdown and a clearer top-100 display.

**🌍 Everyday improvements**

- Gatherable resources and their labels now remain visible in **flat mode**.
- Reduced resource-update stutters and the rendering overhead of floating labels.
- Improved movement recovery after reconnecting, teleporting, or switching characters.
- Hidden combat messages no longer erase general chat. Each channel keeps its own latest 100 messages.
- Settings now uses the available screen width, with a mobile-friendly layout.
- Added a **day/night toggle** to keep the world at midday.
