# v1.12.39 — the sound of the world coming back

- Every sound is back — menus, casts, impacts, the turn clock. The split had silenced them; they're baked into the game for good now.
- One spell card, done right: hover a spell in the fight bar for the full styled readout — cost, range, crit, cooldown, effects, and the aiming hint. The stray right-side panel is gone.
- Absorb effects now say what they absorb: any element.
- Refreshing the page works anywhere — no more 404 on a deep link, and no font flash while it loads.
- Turn-order cards hold a fighter's HP steady through their death animation instead of snapping early.
- The fight herald sword plants again at forming fights.
- Mob textures upload cleanly (no more browser warnings about failed image extraction).
- Equipment that hit a stale-state error now offers a refresh action instead of a dead end.
- Zone music arms once — follower sessions no longer double-start it.
- The encyclopedia gained a third column and now tells the truth about cosmetic types and levels.
- The console is quiet: no localhost calls from prod, no phantom analytics, no shader errors on boot.
