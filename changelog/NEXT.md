# A world shaped by hand

AresRPG has been rebuilt around a deliberate adventure: tailored progression, distinct classes,
meaningful professions, and creatures that fight like they belong in their world.

## A rebuilt adventure

- **Progression is now tailored from beginning to end.** Resources, recipes, equipment tiers,
  monsters, rewards, and world access form one curated journey instead of a sea of generated filler.
- **Eleven professions have their own path.** Gathering unlocks new resources step by step, crafting
  grows from simple recipes to eight-slot mastery, and each profession keeps its own expertise.
- **Combat has been rebuilt as one consistent system.** Players, monsters, dungeons, and the
  Kolizeum now share the same turn rules, effects, movement, and on-chain outcomes.

## New content

- **Yakutia opens at level 20.** Explore six frozen biomes and gather the next three resources for
  farming, herbalism, and mining, each guarded by its own elemental protector.
- **Every class completes its spellbook.** New attacks, poisons, sacrifices, movement tools, buffs,
  critical effects, and class synergies create real build choices.
- **Creatures gain distinct tactics.** Monster families now buff, poison, control, shield, push,
  and coordinate, while bosses demand a plan instead of simply having larger numbers.
- **Dungeons, friends, parties, direct trading, and the Kolizeum** make progression a shared adventure—or a wager.
