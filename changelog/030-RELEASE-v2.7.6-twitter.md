AresRPG — latest update 🛠️

MORI BALANCE
• Bush Fire: both fire and water hits now affect a 2-cell radius, on normal and critical casts.
• Dolly Sacrifice: cooldowns of 3 turns at ranks 1–3 and 2 turns at ranks 4–6.
• Rank 6 Dolly Sacrifice: 2 → 3 AP; normal base damage 41–45 → 30–35. Critical base damage remains 51–55.
• Rank 5 Dolly Sacrifice: critical base damage 31–35 → 35–40.
• Toxic Knowledge: now grants +1, +2, +3, +4, +5 and +10 flat damage at spell ranks 1–6, including critical casts.

COMBAT
• Fixed missing ticks on regeneration and poison effects.
• Shields now reduce poison damage.
• Added a combat map: 51 maps in the rotation.

MARKETPLACE
• The listing window shows the lowest public price for your exact lot size. Selling 10 wheat? Compare against other lots of 10.
• Daily candlestick price charts for newly recorded trading days.

TOURNAMENTS
• Scheduled start times are enforced, including for admins. Anyone can start once the time arrives and enough teams are complete.
• Optional entry fees and maximum registration levels.
• Paid registration shows the exact fee before confirmation. Fees are non-refundable, including withdrawal or cancellation.
• Leveling up after registration doesn’t disqualify your character.
• Open events are easier to spot, and notifications no longer move the tournament card around.

KARES
• Added purchases and claims to the whitelist page, using your registered wallet and showing your remaining allocation.
