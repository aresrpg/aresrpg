# Sound provenance

## Legacy footsteps

`sounds/footstep-1.ogg` through `sounds/footstep-6.ogg` are byte-for-byte restorations of the AresRPG
legacy dapp assets formerly stored at `packages/dapp/src/assets/sound/step1.ogg` through `step6.ogg`.
They remain owner-local AresRPG assets; no external download or transformation entered the repository.

## Sand footsteps

`sounds/footstep-sand-1.ogg` through `sounds/footstep-sand-6.ogg` are renamed, byte-for-byte copies of
Fantozzi's Sand L1–L3 and R1–R3 recordings, downloaded from the
[Fantozzi Footsteps pack](https://opengameart.org/content/fantozzis-footsteps-grasssand-stone).
The pack declares these recordings under CC0; no attribution is required, but this record keeps their
origin and license auditable.
