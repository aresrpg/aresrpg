Spell effects

All twenty spells of the Yogan (bow), the Yajin (shadow, no weapon), the Senshi (sword), the Tomoda (spell book), the Tokei (time) and the Rojin (pickaxe and gold). Each effect is assembled from what the spell does in the game data: element, area, push, steal, buff or debuff. Sound uses the Dofus Retro sounds of the matching class where one exists for the step, and mine where none does. Press Sound to hear it.
