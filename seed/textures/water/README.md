# Water shader reference

The water normal, caustics and noise textures come from
[norio/three-stylized-water](https://github.com/norio/three-stylized-water), the
Three.js port of MatrixRex's Uber Stylized Water. Its Genshin preset is the visual reference.
The accompanying MIT license covers these textures and the adapted shader techniques.

Source files: `public/textures/water/normal3.png`, `caustic2.png`, and `noise1.png`.
Downloaded September 29, 2026. Images are unchanged, renamed for their engine roles.
All three are linear data, not sRGB color textures.
