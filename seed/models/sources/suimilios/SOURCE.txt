Suimilios — curated community character

Open suimilios.blend in Blender 5.2 or newer. The single character scene is the
editable canonical source. Geometry is individually authored for this collection;
only the native male production rig, sockets and shared locomotion were reused.

Visual design
Anime boy with a large face with a compact chamfered chin, short chocolate-brown fringe, flat dark eyes with blue reflections and one small tooth. Charcoal monster-knit sweater, narrow trousers and sneakers. Hair is removable.

Signature ATTACK
Double Cross: a quick right cross followed by a left cross.
The gesture lasts one second at 24 fps. This is an animation asset, not a new damage,
status-effect or ability rule. Runtime gameplay behavior remains in game content.

Production contract
Native 26-bone rig, mixamorig:Head and cape anchors, Rider parented to Hips.
The nondeforming Helmet child of Head authors one fit for all headgear. Hair stays
on Head. Head and hair are uniformly enlarged about the chin; facial proportions,
palettes, non-head anatomy, rig and animation remain intact. The exporter
includes both sockets, and rebuilds copy their authored frames from the reference rig.
IDLE, WALK, RUN, JUMP, JUMP_RUN, FALL, SIT, DANCE and DEATH are native clips.
DEATH remains exactly one second. ATTACK is individually choreographed.
Body and removable head layer export separately. Hair uses the actual exported
Head transform. Flat palettes are packed; three transparent customization masks
retain the four-material contract while preserving this collection's fixed colors.
No external texture files, linked library or scratch workspace is required.

Head proportion review
Senshi measures 29.83% bare Head height after body-only game normalization.
The authored head uses 1.342857x uniform enlargement to approximately 30%.
Helmet uses one independently reviewed uniform scale and rigid placement;
it preserves original equipment proportions. Hair remains on Head.

Supported embedded scripts
- proportions.py applies the authored chin-centered enlargement during a rebuild.
- build_*.py rebuilds this character's geometry from the native rig already in
  this source, reauthors its ATTACK, saves the source and exports both GLBs.
- authoring.py contains mesh/export preparation helpers, not a template character.
- author_attacks.py contains the nine explicitly authored gesture recipes.
- export_curated.py exports the current edited geometry without rebuilding it.
  From seed/models/sources/suimilios, it writes seed/models/characters/suimilios.glb
  and suimilios_hair.glb. Elsewhere it writes beside the source file.
Run a script in Blender's Text Editor or via --python-expr using its packed text.
Do not run a rebuild after manual mesh edits unless replacing those edits is intended.
