Open riding.blend to edit the twelve playable SIT actions and fourteen pet mount sockets.
The Senshi review scene follows the exported contact frames at game scale.

Each character has a RIDING_SIT_<body> action and an editable Rider marker parented to Hips.
Each pet has an editable Mount marker parented to its carrying bone.
Other imported animations remain as reference tracks. Small pets retain their authored size.

Run the packed export_riding.py text from this file to update seed/models/characters and
seed/models/pets. It reads the baked actions and marker transforms. It preserves the canonical
GLBs' geometry, texture masks, skin bindings and unrelated animation data while replacing SIT
and socket data. The four extended clothing rigs are already present in those GLB templates.

author_riding.py retains the initial pose construction helpers. Baked actions and marker objects
are the editable export inputs. Generated renders and intermediate GLBs are not source artifacts.

Suicune's Baked AO point-color attribute includes its cavity and directional shading.
Run export_suicune_ao.py in Blender after editing that attribute. It preserves geometry,
animation, palette variants and mount sockets. The riding exporter preserves this shading too.
