Nogas Cloak — partner cosmetic cloak (NOGAS, "All frog. No gas.")

Open nogas_cloak.blend in Blender 5.2 or newer. The Model scene holds the exported mesh.

Visual design
NOGAS is the world's least inflatable frog: big from the front, paper-thin from the side.
The cloak is that frog worn flat on the back: lime body, tired half-lidded eyes, one straight
mouth line and a thick ink outline, three voxels thick. Both faces carry the frog.

Production contract
Cape-bone-local voxels at the 0.043 unit of the shipped cloaks, in their frame: the mesh hangs
down from the anchor and sits behind the body. One packed palette strip, no variants.

Supported embedded scripts
- build_nogas_cloak.py rebuilds the voxels and the palette.
- export_nogas_cloak.py writes seed/models/equipment/nogas_cloak.glb.

Reference provenance
Brand read from https://nogas.site and the NOGAS presale page on Blast on 2026-11-24.
The reference guided identity; no image is embedded.
