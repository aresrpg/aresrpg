Blast Girl — original identity on the native community body

Open blast_girl_review.blend in Blender 5.2 or newer. This is the editable
canonical source, with packed palettes and all rebuild inputs embedded.

The face and hair retain the actual Blast Girl geometry and palette from
repository commit 43cb5b18a613d3d308e7956772664796204552e0: amber eyes,
tiny nose, curved smile, tapered chin, five forehead locks and orange curls.
The original head is uniformly mapped into the community body's head space;
the outward hair curls are brought closer to the cheeks while retaining depth.
Three shallow stepped temple locks connect the crown to those curls, covering
the scalp gap inherited from the original helmet-era hair. add_shell.py owns
this local correction and reuses the original packed hair palette/material.
The native community body, outfit, rig and locomotion remain unchanged.

Run packed build_blast_girl.py to rebuild and export the body/hair pair.
Packed restore_identity.py consumes blast_identity_geometry.json, an exact
snapshot of the original identity vertices, faces, UVs and material slots.
The packed original palette remains editable. authoring.py and
author_attacks.py supply mesh and animation helpers. Run export_curated.py
to export manual edits without rebuilding. Rebuilding replaces manual edits.
From this canonical directory exports go to seed/models/characters;
elsewhere they go beside the source.

Head owns the face and removable hair. One nondeforming Helmet child of Head
uses uniform scale and rigid placement for all helmets, preserving equipment
proportions; hair remains on Head. Cape follows Neck and Rider follows
Hips. All twelve playable clips remain, and DEATH lasts exactly one second.
The exporter enforces unit animation scales with a 0.0001 numerical-drift
guard and rejects accessors shared with non-scale data.

Verified source rebuild: exact vertices, faces, UVs, material slots and skin
weights; decoded GLB positions, normals, UVs, joints and weights are identical.
Clip durations and unit animation scales are identical after rebuilding.
Original, front, side, back and face renders were compared during review.

validate_hair.py runs during both rebuilding and manual exporting. Its 112
lateral rays check the reported temple region on both sides, preserving the
intentional face opening. The reported mesh failed at 54 scalp samples; the
corrected source passes all 112. Body geometry and animation data are unchanged.
