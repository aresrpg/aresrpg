Ikari male and female voxel character models

Open ikari.blend. The Male and Female Voxel Warrior Study scenes contain the
registered pair. Earlier study scenes remain references. Each sex uses its own canonical
bind frames, Rider socket, native locomotion, seated pose, and one-second death.
Body and hair export separately. Hair and ears follow the head rigidly.

The four packed palette textures contain authored color plus three customization masks.
The packed build_ikari_voxel_warrior.py and build_ikari_female_voxel_warrior.py
texts rebuild the current geometry from the canonical references in this file.
The matching validate texts check head/hair fit and attachment.
Run export_ikari.py to generate candidate body/hair GLBs in work/ikari.
The exporter retains constant pose channels and uses the actual exported head frame
for standalone hair. It exports only the family scene and embeds Draco geometry.

The accepted exports live in seed/models/characters and resolve through the shared
character catalog for both sexes. The attack gesture uses the canonical reference
animation. Female ATTACK_CAC exports as ATTACK; death remains exactly one second.

Checked: front/back/three-quarter views, running and seated articulation, normalized
weights, packed palettes, Rider parent, and one-second death. All ten candidate body
and hair pairs decoded through Three.js with 85 sampled poses per pair. Texture
appearance was checked in Blender; offline Three.js texture decoding was stubbed.
