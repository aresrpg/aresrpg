Yogan male and female character models

Open yogan.blend in Blender 5.2 or newer. The two named model scenes contain editable meshes, packed palettes,
independent playable rigs, removable hair, and native animation actions. No cape is part
of either costume. Existing equipped hats and cloaks use the original attachment frames.

The male and female rigs retain their own Rider socket and folded SIT pose. The body and
hair export separately. Three masks follow the Senshi convention:
  color1: primary cloth
  color2: hair and trim
  color3: secondary leather armor
Skin, eyes, and pale fur remain authored colors. Mask swatches retain shading differences.

ATTACK includes stepping, weight transfer, torso rotation, bow draw, release, and recovery.
The draw hand anchors outside the right cheek; the forearm clears the head throughout the clip.
The spectral bow is rigged animation geometry, not inventory gear. It is hidden in every
other clip. RELEASE is an action pose marker; the exporter derives animation.extras.release_seconds
from that marker. ATTACK and DEATH last one second. Legacy cast actions and the upward shot
remain source references; the runtime export contains only one attack animation.

The female face follows the existing Iyashi female voxel geometry: stepped cheeks,
simple notched eyes, a block nose and small mouth. It fits Yogan's own head envelope,
with stepped pointed ears and the retained layered blonde bob with swept bangs.
Her fitted tunic covers the midriff; narrower arm depth preserves the original rig.
The hair has continuous backing beneath its tapered locks and a compact feathered tuft.
The male retains his broader stepped jaw. Both necks remain connected.
The editable meshes own the current design. Packed build/refine scripts are historical
studies; rerunning them would replace the current face and hair with an older design.
Run validate_yogan_head_fit.py to check fit, skin weights, palette names and sockets.
Run the packed validate_yogan.py text to check head/arm intersections at 48 Hz for both rigs
and reject a disconnected female neck.
Run the packed export_yogan.py text to produce the four candidate GLBs in work/yogan.
The source must stay in this directory so the exporter can find the repository root.
The script exports only this family, retains constant non-rest pose channels such as SIT,
and resolves standalone hair against the actual exported head frame. Blender's imported
bone editing axes are not the runtime attachment frame.

The accepted exports live in seed/models/characters and are registered as the Yogan class pair.
All classes select ATTACK for every cast. Effect travel is independent of the body gesture.
The runtime uses the exported release marker to align ATTACK anticipation with the authored spell windup.
